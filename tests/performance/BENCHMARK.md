# GFN1 stages versus standard xTB on Windows (2026-10-05)

Intel Core i7-14700F, Windows native ifx 2025.2 `/O3`, oneMKL LP64 sequential,
one thread, identical GFN1 parameters and inputs, no fast-math/LTO. Standard
xTB 6.7.1 is the official commit `26b28010e805f7d1aeeef39813feb473e69cc4be`.
One warmup per policy, followed by seven randomized interleaved process-wall
trials with profiling off. Normal CLI startup/output/property work is included.
Separate profiled calls establish which candidate/audit paths actually run.

The stage rows switch explicit policies in the **same current executable**;
they are not archived binaries of each release. The 2.2.6 policy disables both
additional Gradient optimizations, reuse and FOE remain off. The 2.3.2 trials
enable reuse with 24 maximum inner iterations and the earlier Gradient. The
3.0.0 trials retain the new Gradient and enable FOE with minimum AO=1. Forced
trials disable that candidate's runtime speed gate; production does not.

## Median process wall, seconds

| Policy | Disilane, 30 AO | Taxol gas, 350 AO | Taxol ALPB, 350 AO |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Standard 6.7.1 | 0.035494 | 0.315712 | 0.318739 |
| 2.2.6 policy | 0.034046 | 0.220389 | 0.225207 |
| 2.3.2 reuse, autotune | 0.033653 | 0.242779 | 0.227839 |
| 2.3.2 reuse, forced | 0.035016 | 0.253459 | 0.229203 |
| 2.4 Gradient / current default | 0.034177 | 0.218112 | 0.222069 |
| 3.0.0 FOE, autotune | 0.035052 | 0.334022 | 0.336961 |
| 3.0.0 FOE, forced | 0.034908 | 0.756969 | 0.753752 |

Every timed trial converges and matches standard Energy/Gradient. Largest
Energy difference is 9.95e-13 Eh and largest Gradient component difference is
4.01e-15 Eh/bohr. Regression tolerances are 1e-11 Eh and 1e-10 Eh/bohr.

## Adoption decisions

- The current normal path is 1.45× faster than standard for taxol gas and
  1.44× for ALPB on this host. This is separate from the earlier WSL 594-AO
  result, whose coordinates are unavailable.
- Additional Gradient packing/contraction is about 1.0% faster than the
  earlier Gradient policy for gas and 1.4% for ALPB in this batch. These are
  small changes; keep the exact fallback controls and do not extrapolate the
  gain to every molecule. Small-molecule trials are dominated by startup/noise.
- Forced reuse reduces gas full solves from 11 to 9 but is 15% slower than
  the preceding policy. Its autotuned probe costs about 10% here. ALPB rejects
  its first candidate, retaining 11 full solves. **Keep reuse off by default**.
- Forced FOE reduces gas/ALPB full solves from 11 to 8 but costs 3.47×/3.39×
  the current default time. Even autotuned probing costs about 1.53×/1.52×.
  **Keep dense FOE off by default**. The diagonalization-free calculation is
  numerically usable in tested conditions; it has no demonstrated production
  speed advantage at these matrix sizes. Do not adopt it on these results.
- Disilane reuse does not pass the default 128-AO size gate. Its timing row
  does not measure reuse speed. Forced FOE does accept two density-only steps
  but its process-wall difference is too small to establish a crossover.

The normal path includes the baseline removal of unnecessary properties;
full stock WBO/dipole compatibility is outside the project's stated scope.
The comparison measures the requested Energy/Gradient CLI workload, not the
isolated extra algorithm alone. The relative-to-previous columns in the JSON
separate each proposed algorithm's incremental cost.

## Reproduction and evidence

```powershell
.\tests\performance\run_windows_reference.ps1
.\tests\performance\run_windows_comparison.ps1
```

Reference compatibility changes are Windows compiler flag spelling,
HOME/USERPROFILE initialization and unused test-target gating. Its Hessian
OpenMP directives are also suppressed to bypass a Windows ifx descriptor
crash for the **one-thread Hessian** reference; Energy/Gradient algorithms
and parallelism are unchanged. `reference_compatibility.patch` records the
complete diff; active non-directive Fortran in the reference numerical Hessian
is verified identical to the official source.

`STAGE_RESULTS_20261005.json` preserves all seven samples, input/parameter/
executable hashes, parity maxima and actual solver/audit counts.
`SOURCE_MANIFEST_20261005.json` records the current source hashes and reference
modifications. Build-directory logs are regenerated by reruns; the saved JSON
is the dated measurement snapshot. Neither mutable build directories nor
these reports are versioned release ZIPs.

See `../gfn1_fast_4_2_0/BENCHMARK.md` for analytic Hessian measurements against
the standard numerical formula and the current fast numerical implementation.
